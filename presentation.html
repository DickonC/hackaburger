Colour has a job

Build every slide from ink, paper, and a hairline. Sky is the only colour that may leave a chart: as a rule, as a link, or as a full section field, and always as the dark step, #0369A1.

Green, lime, amber, and red report the air. They follow the same cuts as both views: under 750 ppm, under 1,000, under 1,400, and above. Live, model, simulated, and offline use those same hues.
